About Turath

Turath means "heritage" in Arabic. We are a small online marketplace that sells handmade Gulf crafts such as Sadu weaving, silver filigree, oud candles and gift boxes, each made by a named UAE artisan.

Turath is a university project for INT424 (E-Commerce). The products, artisans and orders are sample data, and no real payments or deliveries happen.

How it works

  1. Browse the shop, search by words, filter by price, or use the Gift Finder.
  2. Create an account and add items to your cart.
  3. Check out with cash on delivery. We calculate 5% UAE VAT for you.
  4. After your order arrives you can leave a review.

Delivery, VAT and returns

  • Delivery: free to all seven emirates. Handmade items usually take 2–5 working days.
  • VAT: prices are shown before VAT. 5% VAT is added in the cart and shown on your order.
  • Cancelling: you can cancel an order from "My account" while it is still "Placed".

Your data

We store your name, e-mail, phone, address and orders only to run your orders. We never store card details. You can see and change your details in "My account".
